Clipper

A personal tool that turns my own stream recordings into short clips.

Clipper runs on my own computer. After I finish streaming, it finds the best moments in the recording, lets me review and trim them, and renders vertical and horizontal clips. When I choose to, it uploads a finished clip to my own YouTube channel or sends it to my TikTok drafts, where I finish and post it myself.

It is used only by me, on my own accounts. It is not offered to other users.
