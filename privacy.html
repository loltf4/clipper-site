Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Clipper is a personal desktop tool that runs on its operator's own computer. It has no servers and no user accounts.

What Clipper accesses

When the account owner signs in to TikTok or YouTube through Clipper, Clipper receives access tokens that let it upload videos the owner selects to that owner's account, and read basic profile information to show which account is connected.

Where data is stored

Access tokens, settings and videos are stored only on the operator's own computer. Clipper does not send this data anywhere except to the platform the owner is uploading to, and to Google's Gemini API for analysing the owner's own recordings.

Sharing

Clipper does not sell, share or analyse personal data, and does not collect data about other people.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect an account at any time in Clipper's settings, or revoke access from the TikTok or Google account settings. Deleting Clipper's data folder removes everything it stored.
